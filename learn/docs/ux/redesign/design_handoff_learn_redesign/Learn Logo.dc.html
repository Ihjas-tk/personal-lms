Four half-turns on one axis, each one 11 units further out than the last, drawn as a single continuous line of even weight. One stroke, one motion: the same path every time, further out each time.

What changed from 2a

growthThe turns were unevenly spaced. They now step out by exactly 11 units each, so the white between them is constant and the form reads as one motion.
weightEven 4.0 throughout. The tapering turns read as a mistake rather than a meaning, and an even line is calmer, truer to a single motion, and identical at every size.
the dotDeleted. The heaviest round cap already is the "you are here", and the loose ball made it read as a snail.
opticsRecentred on its own mass rather than its box, so it sits true in a square and beside the wordmark.
smallThe tight inner turn is gone — it closed up to a blob under 24px. One cut now serves every size, from 16px to a wall.
